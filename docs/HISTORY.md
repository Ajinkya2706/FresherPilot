## Nov 14 2026 (late): "freshers welcome" must be supported by the post (PMGlide FDE)

  software development experience", Masters only *preferred*. The AI returned fresh=true with NO quoted evidence (it followed the "Entry level" label),
  and `decide()` skipped every years rule because freshers=true.
- Fix (`llm_filter.decide` + Worker mirror): a post stating 2+ years (explicit_years) with no fresher wording (`has_fresher_text`, `_FRESH_TITLE`) and no
  quoted evidence is rejected `needs_experience` whatever the AI says; a board range starting at 0 ("0-3 years") is the employer's word and exempt; the
  Mid-Senior label rule trusts fresh=true only with evidence / fresher wording / fresher title. explicit_years also reads lost-dash ranges ("0 3 years",
  "6 months 2 years") as their lower bound and ignores "5+ year working relationship". Back-test on 2,008 kept jobs: 2 flipped, 0 applied. Python and
  Worker agree on 1,500 real descriptions (this also caught a `const` reassignment that only fails at runtime).
