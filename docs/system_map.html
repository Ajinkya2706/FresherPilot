How jobs arrive, how the AI decides, how alerts and button taps stay in sync, and how applications get sent. Dashed lines move data; colours show where each part runs. Pick a view below.

The big picture

Readers find jobs, the Cloudflare Worker checks them with AI and alerts you on Telegram, and your laptop app (when open) syncs, applies and does outreach.

What happens to one job

  1. A reader finds it (LinkedIn, Indeed, Telegram, Naukri, ...).
  2. It is stored in D1 as new if no source has seen it before.
  3. Free rules, then AI title / facts / fit decide keep or reject.
  4. Kept jobs get one Telegram alert (dedupe by fingerprint).
  5. You tap Applied / Seen / Not for me, or auto-apply sends it.
  6. Both sides sync, so Telegram /dashboard counts it.

Cron tick, every minute (Worker)

  1. Cheap Telegram "new post?" check.
  2. One source step from the 8-minute cycle (a new Telegram post takes its place).
  3. Every 10 minutes: start the GitHub reader if none is waiting.
  4. One AI step (up to 10 jobs) and up to 6 alerts.

How a job is judged

Cheap free rules first, AI only when needed. Each stage can drop a job; the reason is recorded and shown in /status.

AI provider chain

  • A per-minute 429 only cools that key + model; a daily limit or 401 marks the key out for 30 minutes; all keys out pauses the checker 5 minutes.
  • The second look goes NVIDIA-first (a different model family, saves Groq tokens).
  • Duplicate postings reuse an earlier answer (same title + company, description 85% alike, within 7 days).

Alerts and two-way sync

The cloud is the source of truth for alerts and button taps. The laptop app, while open, syncs about every 40 seconds.

Why only one alert per opening

  • D1 claims keeps fingerprints (company + title words, description hash) of jobs you were shown, for 30 days.
  • A repost in that window gets an alert row with state dup; jobs older than 30 hours get old.
  • Approved jobs without an alert row from the last day are retried.

What /dashboard counts

  • Every job in D1 applied today (IST): Telegram taps, auto-apply, marks from the app's list.
  • Priority = company on the target list (IBM, NTT DATA, Cognizant, ...).
  • Alerts sent, seen, still unmarked, last 7 days and today's list.

Pipeline A: Easy Apply on the board (extension)

LinkedIn Easy Apply, Naukri, Indeed, Wellfound and Internshala. The extension drives your own logged-in Chrome.

Pipeline B: company portals (laptop runner)

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Taleo and similar. A real Chrome window, an AI planner with guard rails, and a per-job memory.

Guard rails

  • check_plan refuses unknown ids, Cancel / Back / Delete, Next / Submit with empty required fields, skills the resume doesn't support.
  • Attempt ledger per portal per 24 h: sign-ins 2, reset mails 1, code mails 6.
  • CAPTCHA and Cloudflare checks are never solved: the job waits for you.

Submit policy (site_submit)

  • verified: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby are submitted; others are filled and left open.
  • all: submit everywhere. never: always leave for you.
  • After "needs you" the runner stays alive and continues in the same window when you answer.

Routing

Every job is classified easysiteneeds_check from evidence (board field, then link host / ATS, then board default). Alerts show 📱 Easy Apply or 💻 Company site.
